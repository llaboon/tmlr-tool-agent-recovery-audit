\paragraph{Class A: partition/arithmetic violations.}
r5-era B2 failures were semantic (safety-stock violations, invalid transfer
totals, insufficient source diversity). The r7 expert batch (20 B2 rows, 12 of
them \emph{small-first} corrections of the ``lower id $\equiv$ larger stock''
prior) resolved the last recoverable instance (8c53cbe8, $0{\to}1$ at r7, paired
gain $+0.125$ $[0.000, 0.375]$, $c_{\text{only}}{=}1$) and partition/arithmetic
behavior is perfect in r7--r10.

\paragraph{Class B: injected-fault recovery (B3).}
r5 scored $0.8125$ on B3-holdout; after the r6 dose increase B3 is 16/16 in every
round, with \emph{zero} discordant tasks across all round pairs and all four fault
groups. Installed recovery behavior was robust to every subsequent dataset change.

\paragraph{Class C: order-reference trial-and-error.}
The residual failure mode of the final baseline: the model probes distractor
orders, is rejected with \texttt{invalid\_transfer\_total}, identifies the correct
order, then exhausts the 8-call budget before \texttt{verify}. 08e7548c traces the
full arc (fails r6/r7; fixed at r8 by reference-recovery rows; dilution regression
at r9, \S\ref{sec:dilution}; restored at r10). c80b3e80 (double distractors,
$d{=}4/\mathrm{pr}{=}3$, distractor quantities 9 and 8 vs.\ target 5) never
passes: r10 terminates at \texttt{max\_turns} with exactly 8 calls, zero parse
errors, per-turn completion tokens $29/28/189/189/187/14/67/51$ --- two probes,
three full prepare-class calls, then short confirmations; \texttt{verify} falls one
turn beyond budget. The B2-dev chronic failure aa74fc8e shows a near-identical
episode signature (same SKU, same termination, tokens
$29/28/185/183/184/14/66/50$), so we classify it in the same class by signature.

\paragraph{Class D: fault-conditioned transcription precision.}
B1-fault stays at $0.667$ in all six rounds, but via two different failure sets.
a334f9e4 and f842c82ad share ids with B1-selection tasks that pass every round:
the deficit is fault response, not task comprehension. r9's fault rows fixed
f842c82ad but broke a334f9e4 (transaction-id transcription error
b0bd$\to$b0bd0bd $\to$ \texttt{transaction\_not\_found} $\to$ retry exhausts the
budget; episode shows \texttt{fault\_group=timeout}, \texttt{repeated\_calls=1},
\texttt{max\_turns} at 8 calls) --- a perfect swap
($c_{\text{only}}{=}b_{\text{only}}{=}1$, net $0.000$). r10 removed the fault rows
and f842c82ad reverted as predicted: id-transcription precision is not teachable
by expert trajectories under a zero-redundancy 8-call budget.

\paragraph{Readout.}
At the final baseline every remaining failure is (i) semantic, not protocol (zero
parse errors in all rounds), (ii) budget-exhaustion-shaped (\texttt{max\_turns} at
exactly 8 calls), and (iii) concentrated in distractor-dense B2 geometry plus the
fault-transcription corner case. Turn-allocation policy under distractor density
and id transcription under fault define the residual gap for a post-SFT stage.
