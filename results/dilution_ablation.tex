\paragraph{Paired evidence chain (B2-test, task level).}
r8 vs.\ r7: $+0.125$ $[0.000, 0.375]$, $c_{\text{only}}{=}1$ (recovery rows
install retry-after-error). r9 vs.\ r8: $-0.125$ $[-0.375, 0.000]$,
$b_{\text{only}}{=}1$ (dilution regression). r9 vs.\ r10: $-0.125$
$[-0.375, 0.000]$, $b_{\text{only}}{=}1$ (r10 restores with fewer rows). r8 vs
r10: $0.000$ with \emph{zero discordant tasks on all five suites} --- a perfect
behavioral tie. The discordant task is the same one in every nonzero transition
(08e7548c), so the regression is one mechanism, not noise. No other suite moves
between r8/r9/r10 except the B1-fault swap (\S\ref{sec:failure-evolution}),
attributable to the fault rows and reverted by r10.

\paragraph{Mechanism.}
The r9 addition of 8 homogeneous first-try rows --- all deficit-4/prio-rank-3, the
exact geometry of the failing task, drawn from only 2 base recipes --- diluted the
reference-recovery exemplar share (8.8\%$\to$7.6\%) while teaching first-try
ordering on precisely the geometry where retry-after-error had to be retained; the
previously fixed task regressed. r10 replaced them with 5 diverse-deficit rows (a
smaller dataset, restored effective dose) and the task recovered with zero
behavioral difference from r8. \emph{Composition beats size}: under a frozen SFT
recipe, dataset growth without exemplar-diversity control is not neutral but
actively risky.

\paragraph{Generator geometry lockout.}
Diversifying \emph{within} the failing geometry is blocked by the task generator:
deficit-4 is hardcoded (workflow.py $\sim$632) over a 2-row, seed-invariant base
pool; distractor quantities 9/8 vs.\ target 5 lock the prio-rank-3 structure;
validation demands $\geq 3$ candidate ids (pr2 unreachable); 24{,}000 seed samples
produced 0 acceptable variants; the sole knob is \texttt{target\_deficit}
(domain $[2, \text{target\_qty})$). Hence r10 diversified across deficits rather
than within $d4/\mathrm{pr}3$.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
Single seed and one run per dataset; the dose response is mapped at only three
points; suite-level CIs are wide at $n{=}8$. The claim rests on the paired
per-task chain and the exact r8$\equiv$r10 behavioral tie, not on marginal
intervals.
